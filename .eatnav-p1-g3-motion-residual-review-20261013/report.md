## Alignment and definitions

At control stamp *t*, the C++ follower computes `[v_x, 0, omega_z]` from pre-physics tile-local pose. The same command is injected before Actor inference and the 50 ms physics interval; `post_root_state` is recorded after `env.step`. For each interval, actual body-forward displacement is the pre-to-post world XY displacement projected onto the **pre-step** body x-axis. Actual yaw displacement is the wrapped difference of root-quaternion yaw. Divide both by 0.05 s for interval-average velocity. Signed residual is command minus actual. This is an interval response, distinct from instantaneous pre-step `root_state[7:9]` velocity. Path arc-length progress is nearest-segment projection on the frozen Path polyline in tile-local XY. Common-goal progress is change in Euclidean distance to the frozen common goal in the same frame. Contact net force and applied joint torque are captured **before** `env.step`; reward is returned **after** the physics step. Their association is time-aligned but does not establish a contact-to-motion causal mechanism.

The first window is exactly steps 0–39 (2.00 s), even for the one Direct trial arriving at 1.95 s. The second includes steps before the follower's arrival step, excludes Goal hold, and therefore has a different duration for each Path. Equal-length candidate comparisons should use the first window; pre-arrival sums are exposure-dependent diagnostics. The interval-derived common-goal progress differs slightly from the established endpoint metric because the former sums pre/post intervals and the latter evaluates the trace's designated 2 s endpoint; both definitions are retained explicitly.

## Four proposed teacher signals

All labels should retain their raw inputs and be computed offline under one frozen version per experiment. Use a common fixed exposure *T* (initially 2 s for comparison with these traces), plus explicit censor/termination/arrival/hold masks. Record pre-arrival summaries separately. For early arrival, keep the Goal zero-command hold through *T* and report active-motion fraction; if terminated before *T*, label the fixed-window metric as censored with observed duration and termination reason rather than inventing a continuation. Never compare different-duration accumulated rewards or loads as if they had equal exposure.

1. **Command-to-motion error.** Define `E_v=mean_active |v_cmd−v_actual|/v_ref` and `E_yaw=mean_active |omega_cmd−omega_actual|/omega_ref`, with references taken from the frozen follower's declared limits (presently 0.3 m/s and 1.6 rad/s) or declared calibration values, not fitted to held-out data. Store the two components, signed residuals, RMSE, commanded/actual displacement, zero-cap duration, and active-command fraction. Report inactive-only cases as NA. Higher-is-better score may be `−(w_v E_v+w_yaw E_y)` with weights fixed **before** outcomes are inspected; retain progress as an independent metric so stopping is not rewarded as efficient motion. Fixed-window raw residuals are computable from current traces; any new scalar weight is not frozen here.
2. **Common-goal progress.** `P_T = ||x_0−g_common|| − ||x_T−g_common||` in tile-local XY. It may be divided by the shared initial goal distance for cross-anchor scale, with denominator and clipping policy frozen before training. Store Path arc progress and local endpoint distance separately. The 2 s raw value is computable now; different Path endpoints and heading caps mean it is an execution outcome, not a pure terrain-response label.
3. **Accumulated realized RL reward.** `R_T = Σ_{t<T} γ^t r_t`, where `r_t` is the **post-step** reward returned by `env.step`. Save `γ` and whether it is per 50 ms step; use γ=1 for undiscounted audit and choose any other discount before collection. The present fixed-2 s undiscounted sums average 15.681/15.595/15.573 for Direct/Left/Right; paired Left/Right differences are −0.086/−0.107. Pre-arrival sums 16.57/44.46/21.74 are not comparable because durations differ. Current trace has total step reward and pre-step Critic value, but **no reward-term decomposition**. Critic value is an estimate and must not substitute for realized reward. Reward manager computes after termination and before command update / next observation (`envs.py:43-63`); the env's reward weights are interpreted within that manager, so do not multiply logged `r_t` by `dt` again. Source `OnixRewardsCfg` includes forward/yaw command tracking, standstill, lateral velocity, flipper terms, support margin, angular posture, action rate, and termination penalty; several nominal terms have zero resolved weight in `__post_init__`. The exact runtime resolved term list/weights and per-step contributions must be logged next time. Command tracking and standstill can favor Path geometry and lower forward caps, while torque/contact-related terms can overlap future Pain measures.
4. **Pain.** Proposed candidate `P_t = Σ_j w_j(|τ_j|/τ_ref,j)^2 + Σ_k w_k(||F_k||/F_ref,k)^2`, with both mean and peak over the window, per-part utilization ratios, and exceedance duration. **No numerical Pain label can be produced now**: neither allowable torque/force, safety factor, nor weights have a verified engineering basis in these traces. `applied_torque` is simulator-applied joint effort, not necessarily measured drive shaft or structural joint torque; `contact_net_forces_w` is a rigid-body net world-frame force, not a contact-point traction/impact or internal stress. Summing torque and force can double-count the same load path. A beam-like axial `F/Aσ_allow` misses bending, shear, torsion, local contact stress, and impact. Allowables must be established per identified joint/body from geometry, material and drive/gear/bearing specifications with documented safety factors, then checked against physical engineering tests. Preserve raw vectors and a separately versioned allowables file; calculate mean and peaks separately, and treat utilization approaching/exceeding 1 as a review flag rather than proven failure without model validation.

## Logging contract for the next collection

Each record needs `(protocol, StateGroup, terrain/mesh/material fingerprint, seed bundle, env ID, episode ID, Path ID/SHA, step index, logical ns, physics substep index if used, pre/post flag, frame, units)` and source/checkpoint/config SHA. Index maps are separate frozen files: `joint_index→joint_name→actuator/drive`, `body_index→body_name→collision shapes`, and any `contact_pair_index→body/terrain shape`. A one-step reward term row must sum to the returned total within a predeclared tolerance. Keep raw data and teacher labels in separately versioned tables to permit recomputation.

| Logged array per env | Rate / shape / dtype | Frame, unit and timing |
|---|---|---|
| Follower, injected, pre/post-noise, post-delay commands | 20 Hz, each `[3]` float32 | body, m/s & rad/s, pre-physics; preserve latency-buffer identity |
| Root pre/post state | 20 Hz, `[13]` each float32 | world pose/quaternion, world linear/angular velocity; capture before/after physics |
| Actor input/action, Critic input/value, DEM history and mask | 20 Hz for changing values; exact shapes from resolved runtime manifest | declared frame, dimensionless or m; source-vs-shared DEM flag, history timestamps |
| Joint actual applied torque, joint command/drive target, position, velocity | 20 Hz `[J]` float32 each (`J=52` here) | joint names/order; N·m, target's native unit, rad, rad/s; pre/post declared |
| Body net contact force | 20 Hz `[B,3]` float32 (`B=60` here) | world N, per body, pre/post declared; not contact-point force |
| Reward total and per-term raw/weighted contributions | 20 Hz `[1+K+K]` float32, `K` resolved at startup | post-physics, reward units; term names/scales, dt semantics |
| Contact point/impulse diagnostic, if supported | 200 Hz physics (`dt=5 ms`), ragged records + capacity/overflow | world point m, normal/tangent N or impulse N·s, shape IDs; startup API validation required |
| Substep body-force peak and impulse integral | 20 Hz `[B,3]` peak + `[B,3]` integral float32 | world N and N·s; retain peak time/substep; cheaper than every substep |

At 100 env × 20 Hz × 1 h, there are 7.2 million env-step rows. Existing verbose JSON traces averaged **16.9 kB/row**, projecting about **122 GB per 100 robot-hours** at the same schema before other artifacts. This is a measurement-based order-of-magnitude estimate, not a tested 100-env throughput. A compact binary base of root pre/post `[26]`, command stages `[12]`, joint torque/target/position/velocity `[208]`, body net force `[180]`, 20 reward floats and ~40 scalar IDs/diagnostics is about **1.9 kB/row ≈13.7 GB/100 robot-hours** uncompressed; the exact Actor/DEM arrays add to that. Retaining one extra `[60,3]` float32 force vector for every 5 ms substep adds **51.8 GB/100 robot-hours** alone. Peak+integral summaries at control rate add two `[60,3]` arrays, about **10.4 GB/100 robot-hours**. Lossless compression and chunking must be measured; do not assume a compression ratio or GPU-memory linear scaling. The observed 4-env batches used about 5.18 GiB device-total GPU, 3.3 GiB host container memory, 13.7–15.4 MB per batch and 53–59 s wall time, which cannot safely predict 100-env peaks. Buffer one bounded chunk on CPU, stream asynchronously with fsync/seal, and run an explicit 100-env resource calibration before a large collection. At 20 Hz, contact-force peaks within 5 ms substeps are unobserved; impact-related Pain requires either substep data or validated peak/integral accumulation, with capacity/full-buffer detection.

## Comparison experiment and next pilot

For all four labels, use the **same** rollout, fixed 2 s comparison window and split by independent StateGroup × seed/terrain; preserve matched Path triplets within a split and do not split env/period rows from the same source condition across train/validation. Keep censor status and observed exposure; stratify terrain and seed. Fit centering/scaling or ECDF only on train, with frozen label sign (`−E_motion`, `+P_goal`, `+R_T`, `−Pain`) and train-only normalization stats. Report candidate-minus-Direct signed paired labels, within-condition spread, rank consistency across independent seeds/terrains, and direct-repeat noise separately. An ablation should compare (A) Path geometry/follower command, (B) A+DEM, (C) B+pre-Path Actor/body state, (D) C+**pre-Path** Critic observation/value. No future Critic value, reward, outcome, or post-Path state may enter evaluator inputs. Use identical train/validation folds, capacity budget and checkpoint rule, and paired held-out rank/error metrics. Incremental gain beyond A, not raw prediction ease, tests whether embodiment/terrain input helps. Do not train on the current single-condition pilot.

The smallest useful next runtime proposal is one **second-graded** StateGroup with two independent seed bundles, each run as three balanced periods × four env = **6 processes / 24 trials**, preceded by a logging-capability and 100-env I/O calibration gate. Maintain the same frozen Path bank and use one Path per env per period. A single new seed triplet can check gross sign replication but cannot estimate seed variability; two seeds are the minimum descriptive step. Freeze new manifests, input/assignment/Path/checkpoint SHAs, reward-term and body/joint maps, effective mass/inertia provenance, contact peak method, resource caps, and new protocol version before rollout. Existing first-differential evidence remains a separate protocol series if instrumentation changes. Approximate simulation runtime using the observed 53–59 s/process is 5–6 minutes for six processes, plus startup, sealing and audit; GPU/RAM/storage caps require calibration. The current 12 trials do not establish terrain-dependent residuals, mechanically normalized Pain, reward-term causality, or the predictive value of DEM/Actor/Critic. The next decision should depend on repeatable residual/label differences beyond geometry-only baseline and measured natural variation, not on local-arrival advantage alone.
