# Portable Evaluator input v2: source fixture and existing-bag parity

## Decision

Pure, read-only source fixtures and three versioned schema proposals are ready for review. **The portable input contract is not ready to freeze**, and neither logging parity execution nor small Evaluator training is ready to start. The limitation is physical field parity and missing pre-Path inputs, not a failure to make the 2-sprocket value resemble the simulator 24-joint value. The latter is a different physical aggregate and remains simulator-only diagnostic.

The isolated worktree `exp/eatnav-portable-input-v2-parity` began at the prior offline review commit `820c965aed1f5ded706076a959a8251d32a43fc6`. [Provenance](source-provenance.json) rechecked 16 prior review ledger entries, 22 prior source files, all 63 sealed trace and summary SHA values, the label SHA, and the three selected bag body/metadata SHA values. It inventories 70 discovered MCAP files (766.8 GB) and SHA-records 48 metadata files. The three prior-audited bags, spanning short primary IMU, no primary IMU, and long primary IMU/arm movement, are the **target bodies** for this analysis; 67 other bodies were path/size inventoried, not hashed or sample-analyzed. Of 48 metadata files, 46 parsed. The nonempty DEM/goal/odometry-like topic scan found only `/teaching_playback/path` in four metadata records. This is not an Evaluator candidate Path. No existing bag, trace, checkpoint or prior artifact was edited.

## I1–I2: contracts and source fixtures

The [Core](portable-core-contract-v2.json), [Core+2S](portable-core-2s-contract-v2.json) and [Core+24J](simulator-core-24j-ablation-contract.json) proposals share exact field shape/order, unit, frame/sign, pre-Path timing, normalization, clip, missing/stale, source and parity metadata; their [field-order hashes](field-order-hashes.json) differ. Core includes four DEM height/mask frames, relative goal, candidate 8×2 Path, deterministic geometry/profile/coverage, body angular velocity, projected gravity, four flipper positions, explicit masks and arm applicability. Core+2S adds **right,left** front/rear arithmetic mean, raw mean clipped to ±100 rad/s, then multiplied by fixed 0.05. Core+24J adds the current Actor's eight main plus sixteen subroller means per side **only in simulation**. Neither 24J nor absent physical data is used to fill a portable field.

The [pure fixture module](../../../src/visualnav_transformer/eat_nav/portable_input_v2.py) uses joint names, not fixed array indices. It rejects duplicate/missing names, invalid shapes, nonfinite vectors, nonbinary masks and stale/headerless fields; `Core+24J` on physical input returns `unavailable_by_design` and no tensor. A full tensor is assembled only if every required field and mask is valid. The saved [simulator anchor fixture](simulator-source-fixture.json) reconstructs its Actor 24J slice within `1e-5` and reports 2S from the same joint vector. The [physical fixture](physical-source-fixture.json) shows available 2S samples while flipper calibration, IMU and absent common fields remain explicitly unavailable. Fixture results do not claim full pre-Path tensor parity.

## I3–I5: physical source and bag findings

The [flipper review](flipper-parity-review.json) confirms RR/RL/FR/FL names, position/order and URDF rotation axes on both sides. Simulator `joint_pos_rel` subtracts a configured zero default; the physical bridge takes raw `/joint_states.position` without a correction. Matching URDF axes do **not** establish physical encoder sign or zero, and neither was fitted from Outcomes. Decision: `sign_or_zero_unverified`.

The [IMU review](imu-parity-review.json) records `/bno055/imu` primary, Livox angular velocity plus `map→chassis/body` TF fallback, bridge receipt-time freshness, and absence of covariance-sentinel checks in its primary selection. Both selected bags containing BNO055 mark orientation and angular covariance with `-1` for **all 79,330** primary samples (463 + 78,867). The third selected bag has no BNO055 messages. The Livox fallback and TF are present, but their extrinsic, validity and exact anchor-time relationship have not been demonstrated. No primary sample is promoted to a validated base-frame angular velocity or projected gravity. Decision: `parity_unverified`.

The read-only [bag review](real-bag-parity-review.json) found 92, 219 and 15,646 JointState messages. All selected samples have the four named sprockets, four flippers and eight arm joints, with finite position/velocity arrays and nonzero header stamps. The right/left 2S values are finite for all selected joint samples. Nearest primary IMU within 50 ms of bag receipt time occurs for 100% of the short and 99.994% of the long bag's JointState messages; these figures measure **potential pairing**, not valid synchronized body-frame data. Nearby `map→chassis/body` TF occurs for roughly 96.7%, 93.2% and 92.9%. Joint median intervals are about 50 ms; the long bag has a maximum interval of 287.8 ms. The short bag has stationary commands; the long bag has stationary/straight/turning **commands**, which are not measured motion labels. The long bag's arm joints move substantially (individual ranges 1.10–3.69 rad on the six rotational joints), so the short bag's near-constant arm readings cannot define a universal stowed pose.

None of the selected bags supplies the Evaluator's pre-Path four-frame DEM, relative goal and candidate Path together. `/teaching_playback/path` is a different path source. Full Core and Core+2S physical tensor parity are therefore **NA**, not zero-filled; Core+24J is `unavailable_by_design` on the robot. The [future minimum bag schema](future-bag-minimum-schema.json) specifies the absent fields, stamps, masks, calibration/status and operational conditions for a later collection approval.

## I6–I8: proposals and next order

The [arm applicability proposal](arm-applicability-contract.proposal.json) excludes eight arm axes from the primary tensor and requires `fixed_stowed_pose` at the pre-Path anchor. The mechanical reference angles and per-axis tolerances remain **operator-specified TBD**; missing/stale/out-of-range means abstain. Existing bag variation is not used to select tolerances.

The [logging parity proposal](logging-parity-execution-proposal.yaml) freezes a candidate state, Path SHA, seed, checkpoint/config SHA, external Zenoh prerequisite, off→on order, one-shot pre-control infrastructure retry only, runtime/resource/storage caps, exact-stamp rules, comparison signals and prospective per-signal numeric limits. It preserves first divergence, cleanup and finalization markers. It is **not ready to execute** because the production logging schema and fixture wiring are not implemented/tested, router identity is not currently verified, and physical parity is open. No 120-step pair was run.

The [small-training ablation plan](small-training-ablation-plan.json) compares constant/direct, geometry-only, Core, Core+2S, simulator-only Core+24J and optional DEM/Path-only under the same independent StateGroup split, train-only scaler, model/head capacity, optimizer, epoch/early-stop rule, seeds and label set. Primary labels are `discounted_reward_2s`, `direct_relative_reward_2s` and `goal_distance_reduction_2s`; preferences/path metrics are auxiliary, terminal Critic/residual stay Phase B-only, and Safety stays disabled. The present seven StateGroups allow pipeline smoke only. Any portable 2S benefit must be judged against Core on future independent validation groups, not against 24J numeric agreement. Evaluator training waits on schema/physical/logging/data gates; NoMaD retraining remains downstream.
