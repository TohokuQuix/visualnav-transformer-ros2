## Pilot measurements and interpretation

Primary, separate fields are local arrival/censoring and arrival time, Path tracking error, recovery timing/type, and body load (roll/pitch, flipper torque/motion, contact and command-to-body response). Common-goal progress is a separate navigation measurement. Compare each curved Path with direct and with its own geometry-only expectations: endpoint displacement, arc length and ideal equal-speed travel time. ±20° have equal arc length and equal endpoint distance to the common goal. A difference explainable only by endpoint geometry or nominal steering does **not** pass the embodiment-aware quality gate; repeatable differences in realized tracking, recovery or body/terrain response beyond direct-duplicate spread are required. The duplicate gate and the identifiability thresholds are those already stated in the design review. The independent units are four `(StateGroup, noise block)` pairs, not time steps or four clones within one process. A pass is case-specific and does not authorize training or full collection.
